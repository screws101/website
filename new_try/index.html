How To Use This Tool

Follow this step-by-step guide to create a meal plan, manage your pantry, and build your grocery list with ease.

Step 1: Go to the Meal Plan page

Each day includes rows for three meals— breakfast, lunch, and dinner—and one for a snack. This will serve as your weekly meal prep guide. To add a meal, hover over a slot in the grid. A plus (+) icon will appear. Click it to go to the Choose a Meal page.

Step 2: The Choose a Meal Page

It will prompt you to either pick an existing recipe or to create a new one. For now let’s just use make a new recipe. This page asks for all of the important details of a recipe. You don’t have to fill out every field if you don’t know it (for example, calories). You can add more steps to your recipe by pressing the plus icon underneath steps. If you would rather not, I suggest copying the link to your recipe and pasting it into the URL section- this way you can refer to your recipe right from the source. After your done click create and this will take you to the recipes page.

Step 3: Recipes Page

Each card is a different recipe, and after the previous step you should be able to find your newly created recipe here. Once you have, click the button that says “Add to Meal Plan” or has the plus icon. A pop up should show up saying that this meal has been added to your meal plan. In the future, once you have archived many recipes, you can click on the “Existing Recipe” button in the Choose a Meal Page instead which will take you to this recipes page.

Step 4: Back to the Meal Plan page

You should be able to see your newly added meal’s name in the cell that you clicked. Clicking the name will take you to the recipe’s inspect page, which allows you to reference all of that important information again (steps, URL, etc.). Clicking on the minus icon underneath the name will remove the meal from the grid. At the bottom there is the “Clear Meal Plan” button, which clears the entire grid when clicked on.

Step 5: Your pantry

This page can keep track of everything in your pantry as well as how much of it is there. To add an item to your pantry, click on the “Add a Pantry Item” button which will take you to the Add an Ingredient Page (very similar to the Create a Recipe page). You can also edit and change the quantity of an item in your pantry. On each card there is an Add to grocery list button. After clicking on it a pop up will appear asking how much you would like to add- enter a number and hit ok. Now go to your grocery list.

Step 6: Grocery List

This page acts as your grocery list. What we did in the last step added that pantry item to our grocery list and how much is needed (which can also be edited by clicking on the pencil icon). You can delete individual items or clear the whole list. There is also an add item button, which when clicked on will ask for the name and quantity to be bought in a pop up.
